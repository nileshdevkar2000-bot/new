SAFETY DATA BOUNDARY

Case-level data stays permission-scoped.

NEO should not expose patient identifiers or unrestricted case-level information to external search. External Google or Gemini tools operate on queries designed to minimize sensitive data, while internal case retrieval remains server-side and role-scoped.
