PRIVACY · SECURITY · RESPONSIBLE AI

Privacy is part of the workflow.

NEO is designed so that identity, permissions, data boundaries, source traceability and human approval travel with the assistant across every department.

01

Permission-aware AI

The assistant cannot expand the user's server-side permissions. Internal retrieval is filtered by role and department before context reaches the response layer.

02

Internal-first knowledge

Controlled records and approved knowledge are searched first. External web search is an explicit, server-controlled capability and internal records are not passed to public search engines.

03

Minimize sensitive data

Clinical and safety workflows should prefer de-identified or minimum-necessary data. Do not place PHI or directly identifying patient data into external AI services unless the deployment has the required contractual and technical controls.

04

Human accountability

AI drafts, triage, risk signals and evidence suggestions remain reviewable. Regulated decisions, submissions and releases require the accountable human workflow gate.

05

Voice privacy

Browser voice mode is ephemeral by default. Live Gemini Voice uses short-lived server-minted ephemeral tokens; the supplied prototype does not persist microphone recordings.

06

Source & model transparency

NEO can display internal and external source chips, keep access metadata in the audit trail, and clearly distinguish generated guidance from controlled records.

CLINICAL & SAFETY

Context without unrestricted patient access

Clinical Trial and Pharmacovigilance are intentionally connected through workflow context, not by giving a generic assistant unrestricted patient-level access. Use authorization, minimization and de-identification as defaults.

Study→Safety→Quality→Regulatory
DEPLOYMENT CONTROL

Enterprise-ready boundaries

Production deployments should add durable sessions, centralized identity/SSO, secrets management, database/WORM audit storage, tenant isolation, encryption, network egress policy, retention schedules, validated AI-provider configuration and formal system validation.

NEO CONTROL MODEL

Identity → permission → knowledge → voice → human.

Every interaction should remain understandable: who asked, what they could access, what sources were used, what the assistant generated, and which human approved the controlled outcome.
