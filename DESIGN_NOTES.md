# NEO design direction

NEO's QMS refresh uses original interaction patterns inspired by public descriptions of modern connected QMS platforms: one quality view, contextual AI inside workflows, connected evidence, role-based permissions, configurable approval paths, and visible auditability.

The design does not copy Ideagen's proprietary interface, copy, assets, or implementation. Public Ideagen materials were used only as a product-pattern reference for the concepts of connected quality, contextual AI, permissions, human approval, and security/privacy-by-design.
