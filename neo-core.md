# NEO internal knowledge

NEO is an evidence-aware operations platform. Its controlled model is Source → NEO → Human → Release.

QMS covers incidents, deviations, CAPA, change control, SOPs, trends and evidence relationships.

QA may review incidents across departments. QC access is limited to QC records unless an administrator role grants broader access.

Audit Command Center has six workflows: front-room request capture, back-room evidence retrieval, exact SOP references, evidence-pack preparation, human SME verification and audit request history.

Regulated or externally released outputs remain subject to authorized human review.
